Good to know

Rekeying vs. replacing: Rekeying is faster and cheaper than replacing a lock. If the lock itself is in good shape, rekeying is almost always the right call.

Moving into a new home? You never know how many copies of the old keys are out there. Rekeying when you move in is one of the smartest things you can do.

Upfront pricing: We give you a quote before any work starts. No surprises, no hidden fees.
