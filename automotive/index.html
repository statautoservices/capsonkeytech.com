What we offer

Automotive services

Car key replacement

Lost all your keys? We cut and program a new key on-site — no tow truck needed. Works for chip keys, fobs, and smart keys.

Transponder programming

We program transponder and proximity keys to communicate with your vehicle's immobilizer system on the spot.

Car lockouts

Locked your keys inside? We'll get you back in quickly and safely — no damage to your vehicle.

Key fob repair & sync

Fob not working? We repair, replace, and re-sync remotes for all makes and models.

Ignition repair & rekey

Worn ignition cylinders, broken keys stuck inside, or ignition switch issues — we handle it all.

Push-to-start systems
