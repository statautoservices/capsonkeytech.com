NASTF Vehicle Security Professional

Capson Key Tech is registered on the National Automotive Service Task Force (NASTF) Vehicle Security Professional Registry. That registration is what lets us obtain key codes and immobilizer programming data directly from the vehicle manufacturer — and holding it requires a background check, state licensing, and carried liability insurance. For a shop, it's the difference between sending a car to the dealer and having it handled in your own bay.
